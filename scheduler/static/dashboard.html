Try this

  1. Add 2 consumers, then Flood acme and Schedule beta. acme stays at its cap of 5; beta's jobs start on time.
  2. Long reports: they never take more than 2 of a consumer's 6 slots.
  3. Kill -9 a busy consumer. Its jobs show as stranded until the lease runs out (~10 s), then another consumer takes them.
  4. Kill one again and press Restart right away: it reclaims its jobs instantly.
  5. Watch the Exactly-once check: lost and duplicates stay at 0.
